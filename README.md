## The tracking rules that decide whether you get paid at all

This is the part most reviews skip, and it's where missed cash back actually comes from. TopCashback's terms are explicit about what voids a tracked purchase:

- **Log in before you click.** If you're not logged into TopCashback when you click through to the store, the transaction can't be tagged to your account.
- **Use the "Continue to Merchant" button.** Clicking a sponsored ad displayed on the TopCashback site does not earn cash back. Only the main continue button does.
- **Finish the purchase in one go.** Click through, then buy immediately. Closing the tab and coming back later — or hopping through a third-party site mid-checkout — can break the tracking chain.
- **Returns kill the cash back.** If you return the item, reverse or amend the sale, no payout. Cash back is a reward for completed purchases, and the confirmation window exists precisely because of this.
- **The paid amount is the retailer's reported commission.** The actual cash back can land higher or lower than the advertised rate, and some retailers calculate it on the pre-tax, pre-shipping amount while others include those fees. The site's displayed rate is a guide, not a guarantee.

Two habits prevent most of this. Disable your ad blocker for the shopping session, and clear cookies for the retailer before clicking through. And if a transaction genuinely doesn't track, it's not lost — TopCashback has a missing cash back claim process where you submit your order confirmation or order number, and confirmed claims get manually credited. Reviews report these resolving within a couple of weeks once documentation is in.
